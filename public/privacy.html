Privacy & limits.

Local workspace

Your imported CSV files, permission grants, runs, reminder drafts, approvals, exports, and activity are stored in the local SQLite database. They are not uploaded to Relay's public preview.

The local database is not encrypted by this application. Protect it with account permissions and disk encryption. Anyone with access to your operating-system account or database can read or change it. Permission checks protect actions through Relay's runtime, not against the owner of the machine.

Drafting

Template runs make no AI request. If you explicitly choose local AI, Relay sends selected due-invoice fields to the loopback or Docker Model Runner endpoint configured by the operator. Cloud endpoints are not supported, and unavailable models do not trigger a cloud fallback.

AI drafts may be inaccurate. Due dates, recorded payment status, and Pi totals come from your register and code. Relay does not verify whether an invoice was paid and does not send reminders.

Hosted accounts and purchases

The public preview calculates sample data in your browser and has no analytics or document-upload service. When Pi integrations are configured, the hosted service stores your app-specific Pi identifier, username, hashed Relay sessions, consented scopes, purchase intents, Pi payment identifiers, transaction hashes, and Testnet access records in Postgres. It does not store your Pi access token, wallet passphrase, documents, or drafts.

Sign-in attempts are rate-limited using a secret-keyed digest of the request IP and a short time window. The raw IP is not stored in that table. Your hosting provider may separately retain ordinary access logs.

Relay tokens stay in browser memory and, where supported, an HttpOnly cookie. Expired sessions and rate-limit buckets are removed during later sign-ins. Purchase records remain until the operator removes them. Contact the operator for hosted data requests. Self-service account deletion is not available yet.

Control and retention

Revoking a grant blocks future agent actions and exports through that grant. It does not erase prior local results or retract files already downloaded. The workspace keeps history across restarts and does not automatically replay interrupted runs.

To remove workspace data, stop Relay and remove its configured database and SQLite sidecar files. There is no in-app account or data-deletion service in this build.

Release status

Pi sign-in and Testnet purchase verification require operator configuration. Real Pi sign-in and transaction verification are pending. Mainnet checkout, signed device activation, third-party agents, email integrations, and SoloHost distribution are not available. The local workspace is a single-operator application.

Pi identifiers are specific to the app and can change when permissions are revoked. Relay does not merge accounts by username or claim that sign-in establishes KYC status.
