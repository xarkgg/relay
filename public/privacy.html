Privacy & limits.

Local workspace

Your imported CSV files, permission grants, runs, reminder drafts, approvals, exports, and activity are stored in the local SQLite database. They are not uploaded to Relay's public preview.

The local database is not encrypted by this application. Protect it with account permissions and disk encryption. Anyone with access to your operating-system account or database can read or change it. Permission checks protect actions through Relay's runtime, not against the owner of the machine.

Drafting

Template runs make no AI request. If you explicitly choose local AI, Relay sends selected due-invoice fields to the loopback or Docker Model Runner endpoint configured by the operator. Cloud endpoints are not supported, and unavailable models do not trigger a cloud fallback.

AI drafts may be inaccurate. Due dates, recorded payment status, and Pi totals come from your register and code. Relay does not verify whether an invoice was paid and does not send reminders.

Public preview

The public site calculates labelled sample data in your browser. It includes no analytics, account system, payment integration, or document-upload service. Your hosting provider may retain ordinary access logs such as IP addresses and request times.

Control and retention

Revoking a grant blocks future agent actions and exports through that grant. It does not erase prior local results or retract files already downloaded. The workspace keeps history across restarts and does not automatically replay interrupted runs.

To remove workspace data, stop Relay and remove its configured database and SQLite sidecar files. There is no in-app account or data-deletion service in this build.

Release status

Pi purchases, signed device entitlements, third-party agents, email integrations, and SoloHost distribution are not available yet. Relay is a single-operator local application.
